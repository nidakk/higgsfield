# Hook & Script Templates

Every Phase 1 (growth) post follows the same five-beat structure from
`docs/strategy.md`:

**Hook → Tension → Relatable proof → Payoff/advice → Loop-close/CTA**

Below is the skeleton, the five hook types the calendar rotates through
(`scripts/content_calendar.py`), and worked examples for each niche.
Swap the bracketed content per the pillar assigned in that day's calendar
row; keep the beat structure and timing fixed.

## Universal script skeleton (~20–25s, matches short-form retention curve)

```
[0-2s]   HOOK — name the exact problem or make a pattern-interrupt claim.
[2-6s]   TENSION — why the obvious fix doesn't work / what's quietly making it worse.
[6-12s]  RELATABLE PROOF — one specific, personal-feeling detail. A number,
         a timeframe, a "the moment I realized."
[12-20s] PAYOFF — the actual, usable fix. Plain language, no fluff.
[20-22s] LOOP-CLOSE / CTA — reason to follow, comment, or save. Not every
         post resolves cleanly — leave some open ("comment X and I'll send
         the rest") to manufacture pull toward the next post.
```

On-screen text should carry the hook line verbatim (sound-off viewers);
voiceover carries the rest. Aesthetic b-roll runs underneath throughout —
it is atmosphere, never the message.

## The five hook types

| hook_type | Pattern | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| `pattern_interrupt` | "Nobody tells you this about—" / "Stop doing [common thing]" | Default opener, use most often |
| `myth_bust` | "[Common belief] is wrong, here's why" | When the pillar is a misconception |
| `confession` | "I did this wrong for [time] until—" | Builds trust/relatability fastest |
| `cliffhanger` | "The #1 mistake is [teaser], I'll explain in a sec" | Deliberately withhold to maximize watch-through |
| `direct_question` | "Why does [specific thing] keep happening to you?" | Invites comments, strong for engagement rate |

## Worked examples per niche

### Moms
- **Pattern interrupt:** "Stop apologizing for a messy house when someone drops by." → Tension: the guilt is trained, not earned. → Proof: "I used to hide toys in the oven before guests came." → Payoff: one line to say instead ("welcome to the lived-in house") + why it resets the interaction. → CTA: "Follow for the script I use when the guilt hits mid-day."
- **Confession:** "I ignored my toddler's meltdowns for a year before I learned this." → the missed cue → the fix (name the feeling before redirecting) → "Try it tonight and tell me what happens."

### Skincare
- **Myth bust:** "'Drink more water' won't fix your dry skin." → the real cause (barrier damage, over-exfoliation) → the fix (one ingredient swap) → "Save this before your next Sephora trip."
- **Cliffhanger:** "The order you apply products in is undoing all of them — I'll show you the fix in 10 seconds." → why order matters → correct order → "Comment ROUTINE and I'll break down mine."

### Self-care
- **Direct question:** "Why do you feel guilty the second you sit down?" → guilt is trained by always-productive culture → proof: "It took me until burnout to notice I hadn't sat still in 3 years." → payoff: a 90-second permission-to-rest reframe → "Follow — part 2 tomorrow is the boundary script that actually works."
- **Pattern interrupt:** "Stop waiting until you're exhausted to rest." → the cost of waiting → a 5-minute preemptive reset → CTA to save for later.

### Hair
- **Confession:** "I heat-styled every day for 5 years — here's what finally stopped the damage." → what wasn't working → the actual fix (not the obvious one) → "Save this before your next wash day."
- **Myth bust:** "Trimming your hair doesn't make it grow faster — here's what actually does." → the real growth blocker → the fix → "Comment your hair type and I'll tell you what to change first."

### Home
- **Pattern interrupt:** "Stop cleaning the whole house — do this one zone instead." → why whole-house resets fail (they don't stick) → the one-zone method → "Follow for zone 2 tomorrow."
- **Direct question:** "Why does your living room never feel calm even when it's clean?" → visual clutter vs. dirt are different problems → the fix (surface rule / color rule) → "Save this for your next declutter session."

## Notes for scripting at scale

- Keep a running swipe file per account of which pillar + hook combos
  over/under-performed (retention, saves, shares) and weight future
  picks toward what's working — the calendar generator rotates evenly by
  default, but the actual daily pick should be adjusted using that data
  once ~2 weeks of posts exist per account.
- Never let the payoff beat be generic ("just be consistent!") — it must
  be a specific, repeatable action, or the "advice so good they can't
  scroll away" effect breaks.
